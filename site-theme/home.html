Guitar Pro in, Guitar Pro out

cecilia reads Guitar Pro 3 to 8 and saves GP5, GPX and GP7/8, so you are never locked in. Bends, slides, harmonics, palm mutes, up to four voices, drum tab and grand staves come through, as do MusicXML and MIDI.

See the score as tab, standard notation or both, with a fretboard and piano that light up as it plays.

Play along with the real recording

Attach an mp3 or flac, or use the one inside a GP7/8 file. Tap the bars to sync it, or let beat detection do it. Then loop the hard passage, slow it down without changing the pitch, and let the speed trainer bring it back up to tempo.

How playing along works

Gregorian chant you edit on the page

Square notation is drawn as you type: neumes, clefs, divisions, custodes, episemata and the drop-cap initial. There is no GABC to compile, though cecilia opens and saves .gabc from GregoBase and Gregorio whenever you want it.

Writing chant in cecilia

Your hands stay on the keyboard

Normal, Insert and Visual modes, counts, macros, marks and an undo tree. The same keys work in the window and in the terminal interface, which runs over SSH too. The mouse works when you want it.
